MANUAL FULL-PROJECT TESTS
-------------------------
Run backend on 127.0.0.1:8000 and frontend on 127.0.0.1:5500.

1. Normal same-floor room route
   - Select two rooms on the same floor.
   - Start Navigation.
   - Confirm only active backend route appears.
   - Confirm no NAV graph nodes/edges are visible.
   - Confirm blue arrows point start -> destination.
   - Inspect every turn for wall crossing.

2. Multi-floor lift route
   - Choose rooms on different floors and Lift mode.
   - Confirm a valid lift is used.
   - Confirm automatic floor switching as checkpoints are advanced.
   - Confirm lift is visibly purple.

3. Multi-floor stairs route
   - Repeat with Stairs mode.
   - Confirm valid staircase transition.
   - Confirm staircase is visibly amber.

4. Blocked lift before route
   - Mark a lift unavailable in Blocked route options.
   - Plan a Lift route.
   - Confirm backend returns an alternate lift or a clear no-route error.

5. Blocked lift during route
   - Advance several checkpoints.
   - Click Route blocked?
   - Select the used lift.
   - Find Alternate Route.
   - Confirm reroute begins from latest confirmed checkpoint.

6. Blocked staircase during route
   - Repeat with stairs.
   - Confirm blocked staircase is not silently reused.

7. Room -> nearest exit
   - Search "exit".
   - Choose Nearest available exit.
   - Confirm /route-to-exit behavior and chosen exit display.

8. Room -> Entrance 1 / Exit
   - Search "exit".
   - Choose Entrance 1 / Exit.
   - Confirm route ends at that exact ground-floor entrance.

9. Room -> Entrance 2 / Exit
   - Repeat for Entrance 2 / Exit.

10. Outdoor GPS nearest entrance
    - Test outside or with browser devtools geolocation.
    - Click Use My Location.
    - Allow location.
    - Confirm nearest entrance name and approximate distance are shown.
    - Confirm UI never claims indoor GPS tracking.

11. Manual checkpoint progression
    - Tap the NEXT checkpoint only after reaching it.
    - Confirm completed line/checkpoints disappear from active route.
    - Confirm progress and remaining metres update.
    - Confirm map centers on confirmed point.

12. Accidental future checkpoint
    - Try tapping a checkpoint more than one step ahead.
    - Confirm it is rejected and user is told to confirm in order.

13. Previous Point
    - Advance two checkpoints.
    - Click Previous Point.
    - Confirm progress goes back exactly one checkpoint and route redraws.

14. Current Point
    - Pan away.
    - Click Current Point.
    - Confirm map returns to latest manually confirmed checkpoint.

15. Floor switching
    - Click every G/F1/F2/F3/F4/F5/F6/F7 button.
    - Confirm each is a clear button with active state.
    - Confirm corresponding map loads.

16. 90 degree clockwise map rendering
    - Compare every rendered floor to source SVG.
    - Confirm blueprint is exactly 90 degrees clockwise.
    - Confirm room labels, corridor, route, dots and arrows stay aligned.
    - Confirm source SVG/database coordinates remain unchanged.

17. Corridor visualization
    - Inspect every floor before routing.
    - Confirm corridors look like pale indoor roads.
    - Confirm no giant circular junction blobs.
    - Confirm room-door spurs do not flood rooms.
    - Confirm architectural walls remain visually above corridor fill.

18. Facility colors
    - Verify rooms are cool neutral.
    - Verify lifts purple.
    - Verify stairs amber.
    - Verify bathrooms teal and bathroom symbol does not cover labels.
    - Verify legend matches actual colors.

19. Route arrows
    - Test long horizontal/vertical/turning routes.
    - Confirm explicit blue arrowheads are visible and face destination.

20. Technical graph hidden
    - Inspect all floors before and during route.
    - Confirm no green debug nodes, blue graph edges or entire NAV graph is visible.

21. Wall-crossing visual inspection
    - Test representative room-to-room routes in every map region on every floor.
    - Inspect every segment.
    - Any wall crossing must be treated as a backend/SVG/data issue; do not hide it by drawing a shortcut.

22. Search
    - Search rooms, labs, offices and facilities.
    - Confirm raw technical IDs are not presented as normal destinations.
    - Search "exit" and confirm friendly exit choices.

23. Pan / zoom / fit
    - Drag map.
    - Mouse-wheel zoom.
    - Click +, -, Fit map.
    - Confirm no clipping/broken centering after rotation.

24. Responsive layout
    - Test desktop and narrow/mobile width.
    - Confirm map remains usable and controls do not cover route.
